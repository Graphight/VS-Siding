# Walls under Roofing

- Status: Draft
- Created: 2026-09-27
- Reflects: mod page request https://mods.vintagestory.at/vssiding#cmt-240611 and the Roofing author's reply (#cmt-241451); `vsroofing-1.5.5.zip`'s assets (no DLL decompiled, as decision 0001); decisions 0001, 0021, 0035; `SidingModSystem.IsHostable`; not yet played

## Summary
A player wants siding walls to fill up to the underside of a Roofing mod roof.
A wall's top is flat at the top of its cell, so a gable end under a sloped roof leaves a stepped triangle of open cells between the last full wall and the roof.
The proposal adds a raked wall: a wall whose top is cut on a slope in its own plane, picked on the saw, which fills that triangle without knowing anything about Roofing.

## Context
**What Roofing ships.**
One block, `roof` (`vsroofing.RoofBlock`, entity `vsroofing.AutoRoofEntity`), with a `side` variant and a `Roofs` dictionary of 16 materials.
Each material lists slopes 1 to 5 (thatch 1 to 4, sod 1 to 3) as separate shape files, and the mod describes itself as "gridless, with dynamic slopes and corners".
So the pitch and position of a roof in a cell live in its block entity, which is closed source.

**Where the gap is.**
Along the eaves the roof slopes across the wall's 4/16 thickness, so a flat-topped wall already meets it, give or take a sliver.
At a gable end the roof slopes along the wall's plane, so each wall cell up the gable is either fully under the roof or partly above it, and the partial cells are left empty.

**Sharing the roof's cell.**
The player also asked for fill inside the roof block's own cell, below its slope.
`roof.json` sets no `sidesolid`, so the block gets vanilla's default of all sides solid unless `RoofBlock` overrides it, and `IsHostable` refuses any block with a solid side.

**What Roofing's author says.**
Roofing's fill and base blocks are chisel blocks, and Roofing will not add another way to supply their shapes (https://mods.vintagestory.at/vssiding#cmt-241451).
The route they named is a wall that can give a chisel block version of itself, though Roofing cannot take a pre-chiselled block yet.

## Design
**A raked wall layout, independent of Roofing.**
A new wall shape whose top edge runs diagonally across the cell, in the wall's plane, generated by `WallShapeGen` (decision 0021) like the existing layouts.
The player picks the pitch and which way it falls on the saw's picker.
Framing, infill and finishes work as on any wall, clipped to the slope.

**Pitches chosen to match Roofing's, measured in game.**
Roofing's slope numbers do not say their rise per block.
Build each slope in a test world, measure it, and offer those pitches, so a raked wall lines up with a Roofing roof without reading its state.
The same pitches suit vanilla stair and slab roofs.

**Sharing the roof's cell comes later, if at all.**
Hosting a wall in the roof's cell (decision 0035) is out: the fill there is Roofing's chisel block, not a wall.
The route that fits is `chiselling-walls`, which already turns a wall into a microblock with its layers' materials, so the wall becomes something Roofing could one day take as fill.
That waits on Roofing accepting pre-chiselled blocks, and on the raked wall being built and played.

## Alternatives considered
- **Read the pitch from Roofing's block entity and fit the wall automatically.** Means depending on the private state of a closed-source mod, which breaks without warning on any Roofing update.
- **Ask Roofing for another way to supply fill shapes.** Its author has declined.
- **Ask players to chisel the gable.** Vanilla cannot chisel a wall today (`chiselling-walls`), and it would be hours of work per gable.
- **Nothing: fill the triangle with vanilla blocks.** What players do now, and what the request is about.

## Consequences & open questions
- Retention: a raked wall's top face is open above the slope, so a gable of raked walls only seals if the roof above it seals.
Check against the room registry, as decision 0015 did for full walls.
- Each pitch and direction is a new shape across every layout it applies to; decide whether `cornerout` needs raked versions or a gable corner is always a full wall.
- A pitch steeper than one block per block spans more than one cell, so a steep gable needs two raked pieces stacked; check Roofing's steepest slope before deciding how far this goes.
- Collision and selection follow the slope, or keep the full framing box as decision 0008 does.
